About the Conference

The conference is associated with the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) Partnerships for Accelerated Innovation and Research (PAIR) Programme at the National Institute of Technology Nagaland.

It aims to provide an academic platform for researchers, faculty members, academicians, research scholars, students and industry professionals to share research findings, exchange ideas and participate in scientific discussions.
