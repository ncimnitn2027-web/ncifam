About the Conference

The National Conference on Interdisciplinary Frontiers in Advanced and Next Generation Materials (NCIFAM-2027), supported under the prestigious Partnerships for Accelerated Innovation and Research (PAIR) program of the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), Government of India, will be hosted by the National Institute of Technology Nagaland from 18th to 20th February 2027.Advanced materials form the backbone of transformative breakthroughs across clean energy, next-generation electronics, healthcare technologies, defense, and sustainable manufacturing.

NCIFAM-2027 is conceived as a premier national platform to bring together leading scientists, academicians, young researchers, and industry professionals to exchange pioneering research, foster cross-institutional collaborations, and accelerate translational innovation.

In alignment with the vision of the ANRF PAIR initiative, the conference places special emphasis on strengthening multi-institutional research ecosystems across Hub and Spoke partner institutions. Over three days, NCIFAM-2027 will feature plenary and keynote lectures by eminent experts, invited talks, technical oral sessions, and poster presentations, providing vibrant opportunities for scientific dialogue, interdisciplinary networking, and mentorship for young scholars

DISCOVER THE REGION

About the City

The State of Nagaland situated in the North-East Region of India is well known worldwide as "Land of Festivals".

Dimapur is the major commercial hub of Nagaland State. Besides being the land of festivals, Nagaland falls under one of the biodiversity hotspots of the world. Nature's bounty and its beauty coupled with the indigenous peace loving, charming, hospitable Naga culture open up a whole new world for the people from within and outside India. The city has good road, rail and air connectivity with various major metropolitan cities in India. Apart from these, the rich historic background of Dimapur associated with Kachari dynasty that existed before the 13th century AD, also attracts the tourists.

Reminiscences of the glory of this kingdom can still be found on the ruins those are scattered in and around the town.

THE HOST INSTITUTION

About the Institute

National Institute of Technology Nagaland

National Institute of Technology Nagaland (NIT Nagaland) was established during the year 2009 by Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Government of India.

The Institute is one among 31 NITs which falls under the ambit of the “Institute of National Importance”. NIT Nagaland is located at Chumukedima, about 14 kilometers from Dimapur, Nagaland. The campus is well connected through all means of communication.

There are six under graduate courses , four post graduate courses in Engineering departments and 2 Msc courses in under Science and Humanities Department , inclusive of PhD studies.
