Participants travelling to NIT Nagaland for NCIFAM-2027 are encouraged to plan their accommodation well in advance. The Institute is located at Chumukedima, near Dimapur, which serves as an important accommodation and transport centre for visitors to the region.

This page will provide relevant information about accommodation arrangements, nearby lodging options and coordination support for conference participants.

Accommodation arrangements and availability are subject to confirmation by the organising committee.

QUICK INFORMATION

Frequently asked
questions.

Important accommodation information for participants attending NCIFAM-2027.

Is accommodation provided by the Institute? +

Accommodation availability and any Institute-related arrangements are subject to confirmation by the organising committee. Please contact the Accommodation Coordination Team for the latest information.

Where should participants look for hotels? +

Participants may explore accommodation in Chumukedima and Dimapur. Please verify the location and onward transport arrangements before booking.

Does conference registration include accommodation? +

Accommodation inclusion, if any, must be confirmed from the official conference registration information. Participants should not assume that accommodation is included in the registration fee.

Who should I contact for accommodation assistance? +

Please contact the Accommodation Coordination Team using the official contact information published in the section above.
