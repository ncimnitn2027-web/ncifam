WELCOME TO NAGALAND

A destination
worth discovering.

The National Institute of Technology Nagaland is located at Chumukedima, near Dimapur. Dimapur serves as an important gateway for visitors travelling to Nagaland by air, rail and road.

Participants attending NCIFAM-2027 are encouraged to plan their journeys in advance and allow sufficient time for onward travel to the Institute.

GETTING HERE

How to reach
NIT Nagaland.

Dimapur is the principal arrival point for visitors travelling to the Institute. Choose the mode of transport that best suits your journey.

01 / AIR ✈

By Air

Dimapur Airport is the nearest airport to NIT Nagaland. The Institute's website indicates that the airport is approximately 13 km from the campus.

NEAREST AIRPORT Dimapur Airport
APPROXIMATE DISTANCE 13 km from the Institute

From the airport, visitors can arrange onward travel to the campus by taxi or other locally available transport.

View Airport Location ↗
02 / RAIL 🚆

By Train

Dimapur Railway Station is connected to the North-East Frontier Railway network and serves as a major rail arrival point for visitors to Nagaland.

NEAREST RAILWAY STATION Dimapur Railway Station
APPROXIMATE DISTANCE 17 km from the Institute

Taxis and other local transport options are available around Dimapur Railway Station for onward journeys.

View Railway Station ↗
03 / ROAD 🚘

By Road

Dimapur is accessible by road from several cities in the North-East. Bus and taxi services connect Dimapur with destinations including Guwahati, Shillong and Kohima.

MAIN ARRIVAL CITY Dimapur
ONWARD JOURNEY Dimapur to Chumukedima

Visitors should confirm road conditions, transport availability and journey timings before departure.

BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Check the
entry requirements.

Visitors should check the applicable entry permit requirements before travelling to Nagaland. Requirements may differ depending on nationality and visitor status.

FOR INDIAN VISITORS

Inner Line Permit (ILP)

Indian visitors are required to obtain an Inner Line Permit (ILP) for entry into Nagaland, as per the current state guidelines.

FOR FOREIGN VISITORS
