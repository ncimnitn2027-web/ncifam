Academic Leadership & Organisation

NCIFAM-2027 brings together distinguished academicians, researchers and professionals to exchange knowledge, encourage interdisciplinary research and strengthen institutional collaborations in advanced and next-generation materials.

The conference is supported under the ANRF Partnerships for Accelerated Innovation and Research (PAIR) Programme and hosted by the National Institute of Technology Nagaland. Its leadership and committees contribute to the academic direction, planning and coordination of the conference.
