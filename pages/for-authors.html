Submission Guidelines

The official abstract template and detailed formatting instructions will be made available here.

Abstract Submission Procedure

  1. Download the official abstract template using the link provided by the organisers.
  2. Complete the template with the required author and research information.
  3. Follow the approved formatting and submission guidelines.
  4. Open the official abstract submission form.
  5. Enter the requested author, institutional and research details.
  6. Upload the completed abstract document and submit the form.
